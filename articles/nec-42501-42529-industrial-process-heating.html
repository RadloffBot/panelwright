(The 425.22(E) companion rule for field-wired conductors to the supplementary devices is 125% of the load served, with the same 50 kW / 100% route.)

EX5 — the 425.14 infrared-lamp section at 120 V

A 4,800 W / 120 V infrared lamp section (40 A) in an industrial occupancy: 425.14 permits series lampholder operation on circuits over 150 V to ground only when the lampholder rating ≥ circuit voltage. At 120 V to ground the over-150-V condition is not triggered — the package is the plain continuous-load circuit:

Where 425.1–425.29 fit

  • The space-heating cousin: the NEC 424.1–424.29 fixed electric space-heating article (60) — Article 424 is the personnel-space heating article (baseboard, duct, boiler, radiant) with the 30 A multi-outlet cap and the 50 A non-dwelling infrared allowance; Article 425 is the industrial process article (boilers, immersion, process air, strip heaters) with no such caps but the 48 A element subdivision and the 120 A / 150 A allowance. 425.1's 2017 scope explicitly carves out "heating and room air conditioning for personnel spaces covered by Article 424" (2023 dropped the exclusion list).
  • The multi-motor hand-off: the NEC 430.24 + 430.53 several-motors article (57) — 425.22(A) (2023) routes motor-operated process heating to "Parts III and IV of Article 430" for the additional overcurrent protection; the 430.24 conductor-sum article is the multi-load companion.
  • The air-conditioning / refrigerating cross-reference: the NEC 440.22 + 440.32 air-conditioning branch-circuit article (50) — 425.2 (2017) / 425.3 + Table 425.3 (2023) sends a compressor-equipped process heater to Article 440 (Parts I–IV), which is the source of the 175% / 125% / 72% rules that article works.
  • The disconnecting-means neighbor: the NEC 430.101–430.113 motor disconnecting-means article (42) — 425.19(A)(2)(1) routes a motor-driven process heater to "Part IX of Article 430" (the 430.109/430.110 disconnect type + rating rules); the 2023 425.19(A)(2)(2) rework drops the 422.34 unit-switch hook in favor of the generic "provided with disconnecting means" + lockable-open form.
  • The OCPD catalog and the conductor table behind every rating above: the NEC 240.6 standard ampere ratings (the 80 A / 35 A / 250 A / 350 A OCPD steps) and the Table 310.16 allowable ampacities (the 4 AWG / 10 AWG / 250 kcmil / 400 kcmil picks), with the 310.15 ampacity adjustments (derating, when more than two current-carrying conductors).
